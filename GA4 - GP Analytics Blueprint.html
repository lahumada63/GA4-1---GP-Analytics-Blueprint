1 GP Analytics Blueprint

1.1 Analytics Blueprint

1.1.1 Client Context

The Restaurant at Kellogg Ranch (RKR) is a full-service restaurant on the Cal Poly Pomona campus, affiliated with the Collins College of Hospitality Management, that earns revenue from dining reservations through OpenTable and from private events. Its primary goal is to increase confirmed reservations by strengthening how guests find RKR through its newsletter, social media, and website, and by improving the dining experience that brings them back.

1.1.1.1 Stakeholder Question 1

How does moving the ‘Reserve a Table’ button to the upper left of RKR’s Collins Concierge section affect reservation clicks and bookings?

KPIs:

  • Newsletter sessions

  • Reservation click rate

Definitions:

  • Newsletter sessions = GA4 sessions with source collins_concierge_[position] / medium email.

  • Reservation click rate = reserve_click events ÷ newsletter sessions × 100.

Breakdowns: Button position, issue date, device

Data source: GA4 via Data Studio; Canva for issue views

Notes: Links must be UTM-tagged in Canva and point to an RKR page. reserve_click is not tracked today (see Part 3). First tagged issues serve as the baseline.

1.1.1.2 Stakeholder Question 2

What aspects of the dining experience should RKR prioritize improving based on guest satisfaction and intention to return?

KPIs:

  • Average Food Quality Rating
  • Average Service Quality Rating
  • Average Atmosphere / Physical Environment Rating
  • Overall Satisfaction Score
  • Intention-to-Return Rate

Definitions:

  • Average Food Quality Rating: Mean of the four food quality items (quality, taste, freshness, and visual appeal), each rated from 1 to 5.

  • Average Service Quality Rating: Mean of the four service quality items (friendliness, attentiveness, professionalism, and promptness), each rated from 1 to 5.

  • Average Atmosphere / Physical Environment Rating: Mean of the four atmosphere items (pleasantness, cleanliness, comfort, and visual appeal), each rated from 1 to 5.

  • Overall Satisfaction Score: Mean response to the overall dining satisfaction question, coded from 1 (Very Dissatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).

  • Intention-to-Return Rate: Number of respondents selecting “Likely” or “Very Likely” divided by the total number of valid responses to the intention-to-return question, multiplied by 100.

Breakdowns:

  • Visit frequency
  • Recency of visit
  • Reservation experience
  • RKR discovery source

Data Source:

  • Qualtrics Customer Experience Survey

Notes:

  • Include only respondents who confirm they have previously dined at RKR.
  • Calculate each dimension’s average using valid responses to its four survey items.
  • Open-ended guest feedback will be reviewed to identify recurring strengths and improvement opportunities.
  • Intention to return measures stated likelihood, not actual repeat reservations.
  • Survey measures are separate from GA4 tracking and will complement the team’s digital marketing KPIs.

1.1.1.3 Stakeholder Question 3

Which Facebook content and messaging strategies are associated with stronger engagement and higher OpenTable reservation activity?

KPI’s:

  • Facebook Engagement Rate

  • Facebook Post Link Clicks

  • OpenTable Reservation Activity

Definitions:

  • Facebook Engagement Rate: Measure the percentage of Facebook views/impressions that result in engagement

  • Formula: Total Engagement ÷ Views/Impressions x 100

  • Post Link Clicks: Number of clicks on links included in Facebook posts

  • OpenTable Reservation Activity: Number of reservations recorded in OpenTable during the same measurement period

Breakdown:

  • Content strategy; messaging strategy; corresponding OpenTable reservation period

Data Source:

  • Sprout Social/ Facebook performance data

  • OpenTable reservation data

Notes:

  • Facebook posts will be categorized by content and messaging strategy and will be compared using engagement rate and post link clicks. The current data already provides post-level Facebook performance metrics that includes engagements and engagement rates.

  • OpenTable reservation activity will be compared with Facebook performance over the same time periods. If direct tracking is available, reservations may be tied to Facebook; otherwise, the analysis will focus on association rather than causation.

1.1.1.4 Stakeholder Question 4

How effectively does the RKR website encourage visitors to explore its menu and dining options?

KPIs:

  • Menu Page Views
  • Menu Exploration Rate
  • Reservation CTA Clicks
  • Website Reservation Click Rate

Definitions:

  • Menu Page Views: Number of page_view events for the RKR menu page, using the normalized page path /rkr/menu.shtml. Repeat views are included.

  • Menu Exploration Rate: RKR website sessions with at least one menu_section_open event or approved menu PDF file_download event ÷ all measured RKR website sessions × 100. Count each qualifying session once, even if the visitor explores multiple sections or PDFs.

  • Reservation CTA Clicks: Number of reserve_click events from approved RKR reservation links or buttons. Repeat activations are included.

  • Website Reservation Click Rate: RKR website sessions with at least one reserve_click event ÷ all measured RKR website sessions × 100. Count each qualifying session once.

Breakdown:

  • Device category; session source / medium; campaign; landing page; menu type (lunch, dinner, beverage, or wine by the bottle); menu section or PDF; reservation CTA location.

Data Source:

  • GA4 website data, reported through Looker Studio or GA4 Explorations. Use page views and enhanced-measurement file downloads alongside the custom menu-section and reservation-click events specified in Part 3.

Notes:

  • Restrict reporting to RKR pages under /rkr/ and approved menu PDF destinations; exclude unrelated CPP website activity. Use the same reporting period and session scope for each rate’s numerator and denominator.

  • Menu views and PDF clicks indicate interest, not proof that visitors read the menu. Reservation clicks indicate intent, not confirmed bookings or completed restaurant visits.

  • Menu-section and reservation-click tracking require implementation and validation. Interactions inside the embedded OpenTable widget need provider support; a widget impression must not be counted as a reservation click.

  • Report menu-section and PDF activity separately for diagnosis, but deduplicate sessions across both when calculating Menu Exploration Rate. Match menu PDF destinations to menu types using an approved lookup.

  • Consent choices and blocked analytics can reduce observed activity. Compare results only after tracking is validated; use initial validated data as the baseline rather than inventing a performance target.

1.1.1.5 Stakeholder Question 5

Which Instagram content and messages get more engagement and reservation-link clicks?

KPIs:

  • Instagram Engagement Rate

  • Instagram Sessions

  • Reservation Click Rate

Definitions:

  • Engagement Rate: (Likes + Comments + Saves + Shares) ÷ Reach × 100 per post. Average the rates for each content category.

  • Instagram Sessions: GA4 sessions with source instagram / medium organic_social.

  • Reservation Click Rate: Instagram sessions with at least one reserve_click ÷ Total Instagram sessions × 100.

Breakdown:

  • Content type; message type; post format; campaign

Data Source:

  • Instagram Insights / Sprout Social

  • GA4 via Data Studio

Notes:

  • Group posts by content and message type and compare engagement from their first seven days.

  • Links need UTM tags, and reservation clicks need tracking set up.

  • A shared bio link cannot show which post brought someone to the website. Reservation clicks do not confirm bookings.

1.2 KPI Tree / Metric Hierarchy

1.2.0.1 North Star Outcome

Increase Confirmed RKR Reservations

The main business goal is to increase confirmed reservations at The Restaurant at Kellogg Ranch (RKR). Confirmed reservations will be measured using OpenTable data if access is available. Until then, reservation link clicks can serve as an indicator of reservation intent rather than completed bookings.

1.2.0.2 KPI Tree Diagram

The KPI tree connects RKR’s reservation goal with four performance areas: Social Media, Newsletter, Website Engagement, and Customer Experience.

1.2.0.3 Diagnostic Dimensions

Digital Marketing: Traffic channel, campaign, landing page, and device category.

Customer Experience: Visit frequency, recency of visit, reservation experience, and awareness/discovery source.

Measurement note: GA4 will support website traffic and reservation-click metrics when tracking is implemented. Social media engagement will be measured using platform analytics, and guest satisfaction and return intention will be measured using Qualtrics. Confirmed reservations will require OpenTable data.

1.3 Event / Parameter Map

1.3.0.1 Instrumentation Scope

This proposed plan connects the website and campaign KPIs in Parts 1–2 to GA4. It requires implementation and validation by the RKR website administrator; it does not imply that these events are already collected. The RKR menu page offers menu PDFs and expandable menu sections, while the homepage embeds an OpenTable reservation widget.

1.3.0.3 Parameter and Campaign Conventions

  • Custom parameters: Use lowercase snake_case strings consistently. menu_type: lunch, dinner, beverage, or wine_bottle; menu_section: a stable category such as starters_salads, entrees, or all; interaction_method: single_section or expand_all; cta_location: an approved placement such as header, homepage, or menu_page; booking_provider: opentable; interaction_type: outbound_link or widget_open. Values are proposed categories; confirm actual controls before implementation.
  • Reporting setup: Register the six custom parameters above plus lead_type as event-scoped custom dimensions when needed in reports. Use GA4’s built-in page, file, device, and session acquisition dimensions for standard fields rather than creating duplicates.
  • Acquisition: Tag incoming campaign links with utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, and utm_content. Retain Part 1’s newsletter source collins_concierge_[position] / medium email; use instagram or facebook / organic_social for organic social links. Store newsletter issue date in campaign and creative/placement in content. Never add UTMs to internal navigation. Device category and session source/medium are GA4 dimensions, not manually repeated event parameters.

1.3.0.4 KPI Calculation and Collection Rules

Menu page views: Count filtered page_view events. Menu exploration rate: RKR sessions with at least one menu_section_open or approved menu file_download ÷ all measured RKR sessions × 100. Count each session once in the numerator. Reservation clicks: Count reserve_click events. For Q1, retain the specified events-per-newsletter-session formula; for Q5, use Instagram sessions containing a reserve_click ÷ Instagram sessions × 100. These measures have different numerators and must be labeled separately.

Respect the site’s consent configuration; blocked or denied collection can reduce observed totals and must not be treated as zero activity. Sanitize URLs and link values before collection; exclude guest names, emails, phone numbers, survey answers, and reservation identifiers. Cross-domain booking attribution requires provider cooperation and compatible tagging; UTMs alone cannot confirm bookings. Qualtrics satisfaction/return-intention measures and social-platform engagement remain in their source systems, not invented GA4 events. A shared Instagram bio URL cannot identify an individual post.

Implementation references: Google event setup, recommended events, event parameters, and enhanced measurement.

1.4 Validation Checklist and Test Cases

  • GTM / collection:
    • In Preview mode, confirm menu_section_open and reserve_click fire once per qualifying action, only on /rkr/ pages.
    • Confirm there is no duplicate page_view from a second Google tag, and that file_download comes only from enhanced measurement.
    • Triggers must ignore section collapses, initial page rendering, and OpenTable widget loads.
    • Tags must follow the site’s consent configuration.
  • GA4 DebugView / Realtime:
    • Confirm each event appears with the exact name in Part 3: page_view, menu_section_open, file_download, reserve_click, and generate_lead once integrated.
    • Required parameters must show approved lowercase snake_case values, with no blanks or “(not set)”.
    • No guest names, emails, phone numbers, or reservation identifiers may appear in any parameter or URL.
  • Reporting sanity:
    • Confirm UTM-tagged newsletter, Instagram, and Facebook visits appear under the correct session source / medium rather than Direct or Referral.
    • Page path, landing page, and device category must populate, and menu views must filter correctly to /rkr/menu.shtml.
    • Registered custom dimensions should show values within 24 to 48 hours.
  • BigQuery export (if enabled):
    • Confirm test events appear in the events_* table for the test date, custom parameters extract correctly from event_params, and test UTMs appear in the traffic source fields.
    • Event counts must match GA4 reports for the same date range.
